# Briggs and base 10

Henry Briggs proposed rebasing Napier's logarithms so that $\log 1 = 0$ and $\log 10 = 1$, giving the common (base-10) logarithm. He published tables in *Arithmetica Logarithmica* (1624).

Base-10 logarithms were the dominant tool for numerical computation — through printed tables and the slide rule — until electronic calculators.
