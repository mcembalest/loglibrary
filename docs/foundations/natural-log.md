# The natural logarithm

The logarithm to base $e = 2.71828\ldots$, written $\ln$.

$$ \ln x = \int_1^x \frac{dt}{t}, \qquad \frac{d}{dx}\ln x = \frac{1}{x}. $$

The base $e$ arises as the area under the hyperbola $1/t$ and as the limit $\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1 + \tfrac{1}{n}\right)^n$.
